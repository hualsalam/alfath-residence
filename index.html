About the Residence

A modern home, close enough to walk to every prayer.

Al Fath Residence was built for the rhythm of life in Madinah. Set in the Al Fath district, it places you a short walk from Masjid Al Fath and minutes from the Prophet’s Mosque, so the day can move gently between rest and worship.

It is made for families and returning pilgrims who want a home of their own in the city — more space and privacy than a hotel room, in a building designed to be lived in for years, not nights.

“Where every day begins close to what matters.”
